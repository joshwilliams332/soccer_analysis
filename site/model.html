How it works

1. Every finished match gives each team a number for chances created and allowed: 70% expected goals (xG), 30% actual goals. Recent matches count more (a match 8 games ago counts half).

2. Each team's attack and defense is compared with the league average for home or away, and pulled toward average early in the season so a few games can't swing it too far.

3. Projected goals = league average × this team's attack × the opponent's defense. Scorelines, win / draw / loss, over 2.5, both teams to score and clean sheets all come from those two numbers.

4. Players to watch: each player's xG and xA per 90 minutes (steadied toward his position's average), times his expected minutes (recent minutes × chance of playing), times how good this matchup is.

It doesn't know about lineups, tactics, fixture congestion, weather or the betting market. Data: Fantasy Premier League (player xG / xA, includes penalties).
